How It Works

Four steps from sea to store

01

Caught & landed

The catch is selected at the landing site early each morning and graded by size and condition.

02

Iced & moved

Packed in ice straight away and carried to the store without breaking the cold chain.

03

Cleaned to order

Tell us how you want it — whole, cleaned, filleted or cut into pieces. Prepared free of charge.

04

Delivered free

Quick and reliable delivery to your door across Al Ahsa, at no extra cost to you.
